About

Photography as observation.

I am Tankut Aydin, a photographer working across street, travel, and documentary photography. I am drawn to everyday encounters, changing light, and the quiet visual rhythms of unfamiliar places.

My photographs are made slowly and observantly. They explore the traces people leave in a city and the small moments that often pass unnoticed.
